The Practice

Gardens made in conversation with their place

I'm a landscape designer and plant consultant based in Salt Lake City, working on residential gardens and as a planting specialist to larger architecture and landscape design firms.

My work is guided by water resilience, native plant communities, and naturalistic planting in the tradition of Piet Oudolf and Dan Pearson — gardens that earn their keep in every season, not just one.
