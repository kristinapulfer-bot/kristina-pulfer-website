Landscape Architecture

With a rapidly expanding portfolio of outdoor built spaces, Kristina Pulfer brings a refined elegance, excellence and rigor in landscape design from concept through construction — realizing resilient, visionary landscapes with every attention to detail thoughtfully considered, the context and function, accessibility, and ecological value across all projects. I design across scales, from small intimate gardens to urban pocket built spaces, from Utah to Australia and across the country. Working as a garden and plant consultant, public speaker centered on water-wise gardens and replacing lawns and nature strips with grass matrix pollinator gardens, as well as an author, photographer, and educator.
