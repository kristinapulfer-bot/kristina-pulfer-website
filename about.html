The Practice

Creating enduring landscapes for a more resilient future.

My practice ignites new ways to knit nature into urban life. I shape beloved places that are socially vibrant and ecologically restorative, combining design excellence with a deep knowledge of ecology to regenerate, connect, and transform the built environment — at every scale, from a single garden bed to civic grounds.

Kristina Pulfer

Landscape Designer — Principal

With a growing portfolio of built work, Kristina brings landscape design from concept through construction — realizing high-performance gardens with attention to detail, context, accessibility, and ecological value, across scales.

Her approach is rooted in gardens made in conversation with their place — water resilience, native plant communities, and naturalistic plantings.
