Services

Kristina Pulfer provides services primarily through her Salt Lake City-based practice, working across residential, civic, and consulting engagements. Her services span the full lifecycle of a planting project, from first site visit to years of established growth.

1. Landscape & Planting Design

Planting design scaled to the site, from a single residential bed to civic grounds.

  • Civic & Public Space Planting: Planting design for public grounds and civic sites, such as the ongoing relationship at Temple Square.
  • Residential Garden Design: Private domestic gardens, from compact urban lots to larger residential properties.
  • Native & Water-Resilient Planting: Hydrozoned, climate-adapted plant palettes designed to thrive on less water as they establish.

2. Full-Service Design Delivery

For individual commissions, Kristina offers a structured, end-to-end process:

  • Site Analysis & Context Study: A thorough evaluation of a site's sun, soil, existing plant life, and water access before any planting plan is drawn.
  • Concept Design & Consultation: Developing a planting concept and layout alongside clients to set the garden's direction.
  • Detailed Planting Plans: Precise plant palettes, layouts, and planting schedules for installation teams.
  • Installation Coordination: Reviewing plant material on site and working with the install crew so a plan survives construction.
  • On-Site Planting Guidance: Staying involved through installation, walking the layout in person before plants go into the ground.

3. Advisory & Plant Consulting

Kristina also works as a specialist consultant, brought in on projects already led by others.

  • Plant Consulting for Firms: Serving as the planting specialist on projects led by larger architecture and landscape architecture firms.
  • Ongoing Garden Advising: A continuing advisory role for established gardens and civic sites, like the ongoing planting relationship at Temple Square.
  • Maintenance Guidance: Setting up a maintenance plan for a garden's first few seasons, and advising homeowners or maintenance crews on ongoing care.

4. Individual Consultations

  • Residential Garden Consult: A single-visit consultation for homeowners not ready for a full planting plan, billed at an hourly rate.
